Thermal Sterilization Kinetics App

Context: Malaysian Palm Oil & Fruit Puree Processing

🔬 The Bioscience Scenario

In food processing labs across Malaysia (e.g., MARDI, MPOB), maintaining strict thermal profiles is critical for food security. Over-heating destroys heat-sensitive vitamins, while under-heating fails to eliminate pathogens like Clostridium botulinum.

This simulator tracks the degradation concentration of a key nutrient/microbe population $C(t)$ over time $t$ (minutes) during high-temperature sterilization.

Inspired by kinetics models found in: "Malaysian Journal of Microbiology (MJM)" & "Journal of Oil Palm Research (JOPR)".

📐 Mathematical Framework

Concentration Function:
$C(t) = 100 \cdot e^{-0.15 \cdot t}$

Where 100 is the initial concentration (%) and $k = 0.15$ is the reaction rate constant at 121°C.

The First Derivative (Rate of Change):
$C'(t) = -15 \cdot e^{-0.15 \cdot t}$

Derived using the Chain Rule: $\frac{d}{dt}[e^{u}] = e^{u} \cdot \frac{du}{dt}$. This gives the exact processing speed at any instant.
